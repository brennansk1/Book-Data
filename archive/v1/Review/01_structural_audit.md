> "The evolution of the capacity to simulate seems to have culminated in subjective consciousness. Why this should have happened is, to me, the most profound mystery facing modern biology."
> -- Richard Dawkins, *The Selfish Gene*

**CH-05 epigraph:**
> "The evolution of the capacity to simulate seems to have culminated in subjective consciousness. Why this should have happened is, to me, the most profound mystery facing modern biology."
> -- Richard Dawkins, *The Selfish Gene*

**Status: CONFIRMED DUPLICATE.** Identical quote, same attribution, same source. Both chapters are in Book I, only two chapters apart.

**Severity: SIGNIFICANT.** Within the same Book, reusing the same epigraph is conspicuous. One of the two should be replaced. CH-03 (Valence) arguably has a stronger claim to this quote (consciousness as mystery relates to the grounding of value). CH-05 (Hard Problems) could use a quote more directly about consciousness, free will, or the limits of knowledge -- perhaps Chalmers, Nagel ("What is it like to be a bat?"), or even a Socratic "I know that I know nothing" variant.

**Resolution target: Phase 6.**
