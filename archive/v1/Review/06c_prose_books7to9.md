# Phase 6c: Prose and Editorial Polish -- Books VII-IX (Chapters 31-48)

**Date:** 2026-04-05
**Scope:** Chapters 31-48 (Book VII: Frontier, Book VIII: Defense, Book IX: Synthesis)
**Method:** Surgical edits for redundancy, paragraph length, weak transitions, hedging excess, and jargon without payoff. No changes to substance, direct quotes, section headings, or epigraphs.

## Summary Statistics

- **Chapters edited:** 18 (CH-31 through CH-48)
- **Total edits made:** ~75 surgical edits
- **Most common issue:** Redundancy within chapters (sentences restating a point already made by adjacent sentences or by the structure of the argument)
- **Second most common:** Paragraph tightening (long runs that could be compressed without losing content)
- **Third:** Hedging excess (phrases like "which are very uncertain" alongside "should be held provisionally")
- **Least common:** Weak transitions and jargon without payoff (these chapters were generally well-transitioned and used technical terms purposefully)

## Notes

- Books VII-IX were generally cleaner than earlier books in the series -- fewer redundancies per chapter, better paragraph discipline.
- The defense chapters (Book VIII) have a naturally repetitive structure (steel-man / agree / disagree / verdict) that could create the impression of redundancy across chapters, but within each chapter the structure works and was left intact.
- CH-48 (The Oath) was the lightest-touch edit -- its deliberate, solemn cadence would be damaged by aggressive compression.
- One typo fix: CH-39, line 52, "This ground morality" -> "This grounds morality."
