### CH-08: Mechanism Design — Engineering the Escape from Moloch
**Status:** REVISED (Second Edition) | **Date:** 2026-07-12 | **Revision:** 2

**Core Argument:** Opens with Alanya, Turkey — an inshore fishery collapsing through the 1970s into Hardin's tragedy, until its fishers, over a decade of trial and error with no economist or regulator involved, built a lottery-and-rotation system for fishing sites that made monitoring a costless byproduct of each fisher's own self-interest. Alanya becomes the chapter's master case and recurring image, replacing the earlier Valencia-centered structure (Valencia and the Yamanaka village of 1930s Japan now appear as supporting cases within the argument rather than as separate sections). Mechanism design is defined as the discipline that redesigns incentive structures rather than exhorting virtue — the Pigovian tax as the founding example. Markets (Friedman) and state regulation (with Scott's *Seeing Like a State* critique of high-modernist legibility) are presented as two incomplete answers; Ostrom's Nobel-winning comparative study supplies the third, with her eight design principles rendered entirely in prose and woven into the Alanya/Valencia/Yamanaka narratives rather than tabulated. Schelling's commitment devices and prediction markets extend the same logic to credibility and to belief. The chapter closes with the master principle — align the selfish move with the social move — and a fully new steel-manned objection: public choice theory (Buchanan and Tullock, "politics without romance") turned back on the mechanism designers themselves, who are self-interested actors no less than the regulators and politicians the discipline distrusts. The answer is Ostrom's own participatory design principle (users revise their own rules) rather than an appeal to designer virtue, applied reflexively to the Position Registry itself; the Honest Ledger then names the residual honestly — this participatory correction works at village scale and weakens badly at national/global scale (carbon taxes, spectrum auctions, platform content rules), a limitation the system carries forward rather than resolves. Closes on Alanya's image and hands off explicitly to Book III: mechanism design engineers institutions the way the next chapters engineer the individual mind.

**Position Swaps:** None (POS-P02 confidence/vulnerability unchanged: STRONG/MEDIUM, but the designer-interest objection is now argued in full, sourced to Buchanan/Tullock by name, rather than merely flagged as needing development).
