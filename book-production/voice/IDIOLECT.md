# Idiolect Sheet — PROVISIONAL (extracted from the Prologue, Gate 3 voice review)

VOICE §2 requires the Showrunner to fill this in; none existed, so this sheet codifies the habits the register-setting drafts actually exhibit, per the Voice Curator's extraction. Every subsequent Drafter reproduces these. Showrunner may replace any entry; replacement applies forward, not retroactively.

**Sentence shapes (two):**
1. The deflating one-word or two-word paragraph after a build ("Seventy.").
2. A short flat declarative, then a longer sentence that complicates or retracts part of it ("It was closer to irritation. I remember thinking, privately, something like this: …").

**Image family:** hydraulics and water under pressure — leaks, holes, walls, climbing totals, flow ("you plug one hole and the water finds a different wall"). Engineering structures (fences, firmware, gates) remain available as the framework's own vocabulary, but reach for water first when describing how traps behave. Do not mix in more than these two families per chapter.

**Joke shape:** understatement, usually at the writer's own expense ("that's on brand for me and I'm not going to pretend otherwise"). Never punching down at the people in the cases.

**Two overused words:** "unreasonable" and "climbing." Let them recur; they are voice, not error.

**Named weakness (used anyway):** impatience first, verification second — the writer reaches a judgment fast, then does the slower work and reports the correction on the page. Named in the Coda as a costly signal.

**Refusals:** never an exclamation mark; never opens a chapter with a quotation; never writes "obviously."

**Rationed construction:** the "X aren't being [vice]; [structural reason]" negation-and-correct move is the book's central rhetorical gesture and its most dangerous tic. Budget: one cluster per chapter (up to three in a single passage where it IS the argument), zero stray instances elsewhere.

**Book-level signature (added at ch-10 freeze):** "coffee gone cold at the confessional hour" is an intentional recurring image — maximum ONCE per chapter, and only at a genuine costly-admission beat. Other chapters may echo the structure (late hour, guard down) with a different physical detail.

**Confession entries (added after ch-01 red team, see reviews/detection-log.md DL-1):** the "this isn't flattering/comfortable" priming move is rationed to once per Part. Default: enter admissions cold, mid-paragraph, or let the reader notice the cost after the fact.
