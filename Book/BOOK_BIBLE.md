## Source Vision

The book builds the **best possible moral system** — called **Constructivist Realism** (working name, subject to revision). The Framework (v12.0) is the seed, not the ceiling. The system:

1. **Grounds ethics** in epistemic duty, valence realism, and game theory
2. **Provides a decision architecture** — heuristic rules by default, consequentialist override in crisis
3. **Derives comprehensive political philosophy** from first principles (8 chapters, not a sidebar)
4. **Derives comprehensive economic philosophy** from first principles (6 chapters, not a sidebar)
5. **Addresses frontier problems** (AI, bioethics, environment, digital rights)
6. **Defeats rival systems** in head-to-head debate (10 dedicated chapters)
7. **Self-corrects** via the Position Registry swap mechanism

The system wins by being the most honest, comprehensive, and empirically grounded — not by ignoring the best ideas from rival traditions.
